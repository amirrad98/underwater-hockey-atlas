# Contributing content

Keep the atlas and wiki connected: every visual entry should lead to useful explanatory content, and each factual article should identify its sources. Prefer a small trustworthy entry over an impressive but unsupported directory.

## Source records

Use stable IDs and canonical URLs. Deduplicate tracking variants and equivalent publisher links before import; do not collapse different document versions. Preserve original editorial copy and source metadata from the source catalogs under `research/`. Record publisher, title, publication/version date, retrieval or check date, language, jurisdiction, access, rights and review status when known. A link that was merely indexed must never become “verified” because it appears in this interface.

Official versioned rules, coaching opinion and local adaptations are different kinds of evidence. Cite the relevant edition, section or page where available. The supplied research notes identify CMAS rulebook v13 (February 2025), but its detailed claims must be checked against that edition before importing summaries. In particular, do not repeat misleading pool-dimension summaries.

## Places and clubs

Use precise coordinates only for a confirmed venue. City-level coordinates must be labelled approximate; unknown venues remain unpinned. Do not infer current leadership, membership or identity from an undated roster or photographs.

For Timber Whales, preserve the supplied September 12, 2026 schedule context: Canfor Leisure Pool, Sundays 17:45–18:45 and Wednesdays 21:00–22:00. The old CUGA PG Aquatic Centre listing is historical. A public roster of 20 is undated; 2026 leadership is unconfirmed. The 2023 revival and evidence of an earlier team by 2009 describe distinct periods. Recheck schedules before treating them as present-day operating information.

## Safety, rights and inclusion

Never provide solo breath-hold training or hyperventilation advice. Refer newcomers to supervised club practice and qualified instruction. Avoid medical claims and distinguish safety context from formal rules. Do not import private personal information, paid manual text or third-party photos. External links do not imply permission to redistribute materials. Keep original diagram labels clear, maintain keyboard focus visibility, and test narrow layouts.

## Before a pull request

Run the production build and content tests. For UI changes, run desktop/mobile browser tests and inspect screenshots. Check all local source IDs and article routes; inspect external source destinations manually when a record changes. State what was actually checked and what remains uncertain. Open a draft PR for review; do not merge or publish without authorization.
